Unix Timestamp Converter

Turn Unix time into a readable date, or a date into Unix time, in any time zone.

Seconds, milliseconds or a date like 2026-11-21 18:30 (read in the time zone below).

Calculations run in your browser. Nothing you enter is stored or sent.

About Unix Timestamp Converter

Unix Timestamp Converter turns a Unix time in seconds or milliseconds into a readable date, and a date back into Unix time. See the result in UTC, India time and several other time zones at once, with the ISO 8601 form and how long ago or from now it is. Tap any value to copy it. It runs on your device.

How to use

  1. Enter your numbers or dates.
  2. Results update instantly as you type.
  3. Change any value to compare scenarios.

Good to know

A 13-digit number is usually milliseconds, a 10-digit one is seconds.
