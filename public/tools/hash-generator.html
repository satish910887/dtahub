Hash and UUID Generator

SHA hashes, HMAC, UUIDs and random tokens made privately in your browser.

UUID and random generator

Secure random.

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you enter is stored or sent.

About Hash and UUID Generator

Hash and UUID Generator creates SHA-1, SHA-256, SHA-384 and SHA-512 hashes of text or a file, with optional HMAC, and makes UUID v4 values and random tokens. It uses your browser's secure crypto functions, so nothing leaves your device.

How to use

  1. Pick your options or enter a value.
  2. The result updates instantly.
  3. Tap a result to copy it.

Good to know

Prefer SHA-256 or higher. Do not use plain hashes to store passwords.
