Split PDF

Split.

In ranges mode, each comma-separated part becomes its own PDF.

Tap pages below to select them.

Your files are processed on your device and never uploaded. Limits: 30 MB per image, 50 MB per PDF.

About Split PDF

Split PDF extracts pages into one PDF, makes a separate PDF for each range, splits every page or every N pages into a ZIP, or keeps only odd or even pages. Tap page thumbnails to select pages instead of typing ranges.

How to use

  1. Tap the box (or drag a file onto it) and choose your file.
  2. Adjust the options if you need to.
  3. Download the result. Your file never leaves your device.

Good to know

Use Separate PDF for each range to cut a long file into chapters.
