Organize PDF Pages

Reorder, delete, rotate or reverse PDF pages without uploading.

Your files are processed on your device and never uploaded. Limits: 30 MB per image, 50 MB per PDF.

About Organize PDF Pages

Organize PDF Pages lets you change the order of pages, delete pages, rotate chosen pages or reverse the whole document. Type page numbers and ranges such as 3, 1, 2, 5-7. The PDF is processed in your browser and never uploaded.

How to use

  1. Tap the box (or drag a file onto it) and choose your file.
  2. Adjust the options if you need to.
  3. Download the result. Your file never leaves your device.

Good to know

Keep the original file safe. The result is saved as a new PDF.
