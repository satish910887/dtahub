About Image Editor

Image Editor crops with a draggable box and fixed shapes such as 1:1 or 16:9, rotates, flips and straightens, adjusts exposure, contrast, highlights, shadows, warmth and more, and applies filters and effects. Draw with a pen, arrows, boxes and ovals, add text in any language, and hide faces, numbers or text with a block blur that is baked into the saved file. Compare before and after, undo and redo any step, then save as PNG, JPG or WebP, or copy the image. Pro tools add curves, a colour mix for eight hues, clarity, a retouch brush (heal spots, smooth skin, lighten, darken), round or band focus blur, background removal for plain backgrounds, frames with round corners, fonts and emoji stickers. Everything runs on your device.

How to use

  1. Tap the box (or drag a file onto it) and choose your file.
  2. Adjust the options if you need to.
  3. Download the result. Your file never leaves your device.

Good to know

Double-tap a slider to set it back to zero, and use Auto enhance as a quick start.
