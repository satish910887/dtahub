Document Scanner

Turn photos of paper into clean, straight scans and a PDF.

1. Drag the corners

2. Choose a look

3. Save

Up to 20 pages.

Your files are processed on your device and never uploaded. Limits: 30 MB per image, 50 MB per PDF.

About Document Scanner

Document Scanner turns phone photos of paper into straight, clean scans. Drag the four corners to the page edges (or use auto-detect), pick a look such as clean colour, grey or black and white, and save many pages as one PDF. There is no watermark, no sign-up, and your photos never leave your device.

How to use

  1. Tap the box (or drag a file onto it) and choose your file.
  2. Adjust the options if you need to.
  3. Download the result. Your file never leaves your device.

Good to know

Take the photo in good light with the page flat. Dark table behind a white page works best for auto-detect.
