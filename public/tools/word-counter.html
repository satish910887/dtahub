About Word Counter

Word Counter counts words, characters with and without spaces, sentences, paragraphs and estimated reading time. It understands Hindi full stops as well as English ones.

How to use

  1. Type or paste your text into the box.
  2. Tap the action button for the result.
  3. Copy the result with one tap.

Good to know

Reading time assumes about 200 words per minute.
