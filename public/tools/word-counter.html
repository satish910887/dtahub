About Word Counter

Word Counter shows words, characters, sentences, paragraphs, lines, unique words, average and longest word, reading and speaking time, and your top keywords with their share of the text. It understands Hindi as well as English, and can check your text against limits such as 280 characters for a tweet.

How to use

  1. Type or paste your text into the box.
  2. Tap the action button for the result.
  3. Copy the result with one tap.

Good to know

Pick a limit to watch the bar turn red when you go over.
