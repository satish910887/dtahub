About Image Converter

Image Converter changes photos between JPG, PNG, WebP and AVIF when your browser supports it. Convert many files at once, choose the quality, pick a background colour for transparent areas when saving as JPG, and download everything as a ZIP. Add an optional text watermark in any language, and note that converting removes hidden photo data such as GPS location.

How to use

  1. Tap the box (or drag a file onto it) and choose your file.
  2. Adjust the options if you need to.
  3. Download the result. Your file never leaves your device.

Good to know

WebP is usually much smaller than JPG at the same look.
