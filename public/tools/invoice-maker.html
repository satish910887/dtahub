About Invoice Maker

Invoice Maker creates a clean invoice with your details, your client, line items, optional GST per item and a discount. It totals everything, writes the amount in words and lets you save the invoice as a PDF. Your details stay on your device. It does not file GST returns or replace an accountant.

How to use

  1. Fill in the details. They are saved on your device as you type.
  2. Check the live preview on the page.
  3. Tap Download PDF and choose Save as PDF.

Good to know

Number your invoices in order and check the GST rate and your GSTIN with your accountant.
