Password Generator

Strong random passwords made on your device. Nothing is saved or sent.

Your text stays in your browser. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere.

About Password Generator

Password Generator creates strong random passwords up to 64 characters, or easy-to-remember passphrases from words. Choose character types, avoid look-alike characters, make several at once and see a strength meter with an estimated time to crack. Everything runs on your device.

How to use

  1. Type or paste your text into the box.
  2. Tap the action button for the result.
  3. Copy the result with one tap.

Good to know

Longer beats complex: a 6-word passphrase is strong and easy to type.
