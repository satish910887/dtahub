About Cover Letter Maker

Cover Letter Maker turns a few short answers into a clean one-page letter. It fills your name and contact details from your saved resume, offers three tones, and lets you edit the final text before you save it as a PDF. It uses a template, not artificial intelligence, so read and personalise the letter before you send it.

How to use

  1. Fill in the details. They are saved on your device as you type.
  2. Check the live preview on the page.
  3. Tap Download PDF and choose Save as PDF.

Good to know

Name the company and the role, and mention one real result you achieved. Keep the letter under one page.
