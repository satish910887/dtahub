About Regex Tester

Regex Tester runs a regular expression on your sample text and highlights every match, with numbered and named groups. Toggle flags and try a replacement with $1 style references. It uses your browser's own JavaScript engine.

How to use

  1. Type or paste your text into the box.
  2. Tap the action button for the result.
  3. Copy the result with one tap.

Good to know

Start simple and add parts one at a time. Anchors ^ and $ need the m flag for each line.
