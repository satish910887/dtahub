About Compress Image

Compress Image reduces the file size of JPG, PNG and WebP photos. Move the quality slider and watch the size change live, then download. Useful for forms that accept only 100 KB or 200 KB and for sending photos on slow networks.

How to use

  1. Tap the box (or drag a file onto it) and choose your file.
  2. Adjust the options if you need to.
  3. Download the result. Your file never leaves your device.

Good to know

Quality 60 to 80 percent usually looks the same. Still too big? Resize the image first.
