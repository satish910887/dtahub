Resize Image

Change the width and height of a photo.

Your files are processed on your device and never uploaded. Limits: 30 MB per image, 50 MB per PDF.

About Resize Image

Resize Image changes the width and height of one or many photos by exact pixels, percentage, centimetres, millimetres or inches with a DPI setting. Pick presets for Instagram, YouTube, WhatsApp or passport photos, choose stretch, fit or fill with a background colour, and download a ZIP.

How to use

  1. Tap the box (or drag a file onto it) and choose your file.
  2. Adjust the options if you need to.
  3. Download the result. Your file never leaves your device.

Good to know

For printing, pick cm or inch and set 300 DPI.
