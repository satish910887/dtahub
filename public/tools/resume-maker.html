Resume Maker

Build a professional resume with a live preview and save it as a PDF. Your details stay on your device.

Many companies and ATS prefer resumes without a photo. The Classic, Minimal and ATS Simple templates do not show it.

Saved versions

Keep a separate copy for each job, for example Accountant and Sales.

    In the print window choose “Save as PDF” as the printer. The PDF keeps real, selectable text.

      Your details are saved only on this device while you type. Nothing is uploaded. Use Export to keep a backup file.

      About Resume Maker

      Resume Maker lets you build a professional resume with six original templates (including an ATS-simple and an Indian fresher CV), a live A4 preview, your own accent colour and font, an optional photo, drag-and-drop ordering of sections and saved versions for different jobs. The checklist flags a missing summary, thin experience or a resume that runs past two pages. The PDF keeps real selectable text so applicant tracking systems can read it.

      How to use

      1. Fill in your details section by section. Everything is saved on your device as you type.
      2. Pick a template, colour and font and watch the live preview.
      3. Check the checklist, then tap Download PDF and choose Save as PDF.

      Good to know

      Keep it to one or two pages, start each point with an action word and add numbers where you can. Use Export to keep a backup file.
