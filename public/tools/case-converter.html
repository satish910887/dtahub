About Case Converter

Case Converter changes text to UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, Sentence case, tOGGLE and aLtErNaTiNg, plus camelCase, PascalCase, snake_case, kebab-case, CONSTANT_CASE, dot.case and URL slugs. Clean-up tools trim and remove extra spaces, blank or duplicate lines, and sort or reverse lines.

How to use

  1. Type or paste your text into the box.
  2. Tap the action button for the result.
  3. Copy the result with one tap.

Good to know

Pasted a list? Use Remove duplicate lines, then Sort lines.
