About Colour Palette Generator

Colour Palette Generator builds complementary, split complementary, analogous, triadic, tetradic, monochromatic and tint-and-shade palettes from one colour. It checks text contrast against WCAG AA and AAA, picks the main colours from a photo on your device, and exports the palette as CSS or SCSS variables, a Tailwind config or JSON.

How to use

  1. Pick your options or enter a value.
  2. The result updates instantly.
  3. Tap a result to copy it.

Good to know

For readable body text aim for a contrast ratio of at least 4.5 to 1.
