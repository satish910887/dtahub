About Colour Palette Generator

Colour Palette Generator builds matching colours from one base colour: complementary, analogous, triadic and a row of tints and shades. Tap any swatch to copy its HEX code, and see its RGB and HSL values and its contrast ratio against white and black.

How to use

  1. Pick your options or enter a value.
  2. The result updates instantly.
  3. Tap a result to copy it.

Good to know

For readable text aim for a contrast ratio of at least 4.5 to 1.
