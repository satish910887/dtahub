    About Meta Tag Generator

    Meta Tag Generator builds title, description, canonical, robots, Open Graph, X (Twitter) and optional JSON-LD tags. It checks your title and description length, shows how the page may look in Google and when shared, and lets you copy the tags or download a ready HTML snippet. Everything runs in your browser.

    How to use

    1. Type or paste your text into the box.
    2. Tap the action button for the result.
    3. Copy the result with one tap.

    Good to know

    Keep titles to about 60 characters and descriptions to about 160 so they are not cut off.
