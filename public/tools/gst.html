About GST Calculator

GST Calculator adds or removes GST at 0.25, 3, 5, 12, 18, 28 percent or any custom rate, and splits it into CGST and SGST for same-state sales or IGST for other-state sales. It can round the total, and an itemised bill handles many items at different rates with a CSV download.

How to use

  1. Enter your numbers or dates.
  2. Results update instantly as you type.
  3. Change any value to compare scenarios.

Good to know

For mixed-rate invoices, use the itemised bill so each rate is totalled separately.
