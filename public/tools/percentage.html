Percentage Calculator

Quick percentage maths.

Calculations run in your browser. Nothing you enter is stored or sent.

About Percentage Calculator

Percentage Calculator has six modes: X percent of Y, X is what percent of Y, percentage change, add or subtract a percentage, find the whole from a part, and percentage difference. Each answer shows how it was worked out, and recent calculations are kept on your device.

How to use

  1. Enter your numbers or dates.
  2. Results update instantly as you type.
  3. Change any value to compare scenarios.

Good to know

Read the small working line under each answer to check the formula used.
