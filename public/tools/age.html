About Age Calculator

Age Calculator gives your exact age in years, months and days, total months, weeks, days and hours, the weekday you were born, your Sun sign and a countdown to your next birthday. It handles 29 February births, can find age on any date, can compare two people, and can copy a one-line summary of the result.

How to use

  1. Enter your numbers or dates.
  2. Results update instantly as you type.
  3. Change any value to compare scenarios.

Good to know

Change the second date to find age on a form's cut-off date.
