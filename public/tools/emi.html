About EMI Calculator

EMI Calculator finds your monthly loan instalment, total interest and total payment, with a balance chart and a year-by-year table. Add a monthly extra payment or a one-time prepayment to see how much interest you save and how many months sooner the loan ends, and compare tenures side by side.

How to use

  1. Enter your numbers or dates.
  2. Results update instantly as you type.
  3. Change any value to compare scenarios.

Good to know

Use the extra-payment box to see how small prepayments cut years off a loan.
