PDF Editor: Fill, Sign, Annotate

Fill forms, sign, highlight, redact, stamp and annotate any PDF, then download.

Sign with your finger or mouse in the box.

More options: watermark, page numbers, details

English text uses a standard font. Hindi text is added as an image so it looks right. Whiteout only covers what is under it. To really remove text, use Redact with the permanent option. Your PDF never leaves your device.

Your files are processed on your device and never uploaded. Limits: 30 MB per image, 50 MB per PDF.

About PDF Editor: Fill, Sign, Annotate

PDF Editor opens any PDF and lets you fill its form fields directly, type text anywhere, add ticks, dates, stamps, images and your own drawn signature, highlight, white out or black out areas, draw boxes, ovals, lines, arrows and freehand, and add a watermark or page numbers to every page. Redact can permanently remove the text underneath. Rotated pages work, edits can be undone, and Hindi text is supported. Your file is processed on your device and is never uploaded.

How to use

  1. Tap the box (or drag a file onto it) and choose your file.
  2. Adjust the options if you need to.
  3. Download the result. Your file never leaves your device.

Good to know

Zoom in on your phone for exact placement. Use the move handle on a selected item to drag it.
