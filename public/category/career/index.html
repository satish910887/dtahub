About Resume & Career

A good resume is short, clear and easy for both people and software to read. The DTAhub resume maker gives you six original templates, a live A4 preview and a checklist that points out what is missing. Your details are saved only on your own device and the PDF keeps real, selectable text, which is what most applicant tracking systems need.
