Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Your files stay on your device

DTAhub tools (PDF, image, text, calculators and others) run inside your browser. Your photos, PDFs, documents and text are processed on your own device and are not uploaded to our servers.

What we store on your device

We save a few things in your browser's local storage so the site works the way you left it: your theme and accent colour, language, animation choice, favourite and recently used tools, drafts of a resume, invoice or cover letter you chose to save, your recent Assistant conversation, and a small counter that limits how often the contact form can be used. We also keep a copy of pages you open in your browser cache so they work offline.

None of this is sent to us. You can see and delete it any time from the Settings page or by clearing this site's data in your browser. We do not ask you to create an account.

The DTAhub Assistant

The Assistant runs entirely in your browser using code and data shipped with the site. What you type to it is not sent to any server or to any outside AI service.

Analytics and advertising

At the time of writing DTAhub does not run analytics, does not show ads and does not set advertising or tracking cookies. If we add privacy-friendly visit counting or ads (for example Google AdSense) in the future, we will update this page before they start and explain what they collect and how to opt out.

Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, may keep standard technical server logs such as IP address and requested page for security and reliability. We do not use them to identify you.

Contact form

If you send a message through the Contact page, the name, email and message you type are sent to our form provider Web3Forms, which delivers them to us so we can read and reply. Please do not include passwords or sensitive personal details. We use your details only to respond to you.

Children

DTAhub is a general-purpose toolkit and is not directed at young children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Questions and changes

We may update this policy as the site changes, and the date above will change with it. For privacy questions or to report a security issue, use the Contact page.
