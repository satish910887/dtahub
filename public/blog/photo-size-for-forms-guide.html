How to reduce photo size for online forms (50 KB, 100 KB)

Many application forms ask for a photo under a size such as 50 KB or 100 KB, and sometimes in exact pixels. Do it in two steps: resize first, then compress.

Step 1: set the dimensions

  1. Open Resize Image and add your photo.
  2. Enter the width and height the form asks for. Use pixels, or cm with a DPI setting if the form gives centimetres.
  3. Download the resized photo.

Step 2: reduce the file size

  1. Open Compress Image and add the resized photo.
  2. Set the target size, for example 50 KB or 100 KB, or lower the quality a little.
  3. Download and check that the size is under the limit.

Tips

  • Use JPG for photos. It is usually much smaller than PNG.
  • Resize before compressing. Fewer pixels means a smaller file with less quality loss.
  • Do not enlarge a small photo. It gets blurry.
  • Always check the form's exact rules (format, size, dimensions), because each form is different.

Your photos are processed on your phone and never uploaded.
