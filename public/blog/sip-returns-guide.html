How to estimate your SIP returns

A SIP (systematic investment plan) invests a fixed amount in a mutual fund every month. Nobody can promise the return, but you can estimate what a steady return would give.

The formula

Future value = P × [((1 + i)^n − 1) ÷ i] × (1 + i). Here P is the monthly amount, i is the yearly rate divided by 12 and by 100, and n is the number of months.

Example

₹5,000 a month at 12 percent a year for 10 years means you invest ₹6,00,000. At a steady 12 percent the estimate is about ₹11.6 lakh.

Steps

  1. Open the SIP Calculator.
  2. Enter your monthly amount, the return you expect and the number of years.
  3. Optionally add a yearly increase if you plan to raise your SIP.
  4. Read the invested amount, estimated gain and the year by year table.

Good to know

  • Real returns go up and down every year. Try a lower rate too, such as 8 or 10 percent.
  • The estimate ignores taxes, fund charges and inflation.
  • This is an estimate, not financial advice. Check with a qualified adviser before investing.
