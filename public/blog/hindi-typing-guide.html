How to type Hindi using English letters

You do not need a Hindi keyboard. Type the sound in English letters and the Hindi Typing Helper turns it into Devanagari. It works offline.

Basic example

Type: namaste dosto. You get: नमस्ते दोस्तो.

Rules to remember

  • Long vowels: aa = आ/ा, ee = ई/ी, oo = ऊ/ू, ai = ऐ/ै, au = औ/ौ.
  • Capital T, D, N, Th, Dh give ट ड ण ठ ढ. Small t, d, n, th, dh give त द न थ ध.
  • M gives ं, H gives ः, ~ gives ँ and the | key gives ।.
  • A word ending in ain or oon gets ं automatically: hain = हैं, hoon = हूं.

More examples

  • kitaab = किताब
  • vidyaalay = विद्यालय
  • shikshak = शिक्षक
  • kshetr = क्षेत्र

Using the result

Tap Copy and paste the Hindi text into WhatsApp, a document or a form. If a word comes out slightly different, edit it in the box. Your text stays in your browser.
