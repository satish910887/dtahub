How to make an ATS-friendly resume for free

Many companies use software called an applicant tracking system (ATS) to store and search resumes. A clear, simple resume is easier for both that software and the recruiter to read. You can build one free on your phone.

Steps

  1. Open Resume Maker and choose the ATS Simple template.
  2. Write a 2 to 3 line summary with your role and your strongest skill.
  3. Under each job, add 3 to 5 points. Start each with an action word and add numbers where you can.
  4. List the skills that the job post asks for, but only the ones you really have.
  5. Check the checklist, then tap Download PDF and choose Save as PDF.

Tips

  • Keep it to one page, or two if you have many years of experience.
  • Use plain headings such as Experience, Education and Skills.
  • Avoid putting text inside images. The PDF from Resume Maker keeps real, selectable text.
  • Add a photo only if the employer asks for one.
  • Be truthful. Interviewers will ask about everything you write.
