Frequently asked questions

Is DTAhub really free?

Yes. Every tool is free to use, with no sign-up, no watermark and no hidden limits.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. PDF, image, text and calculator tools run inside your own browser. Your files and text never leave your device.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Your theme, language, favourites and recently used tools are saved only on your own device.

Can I use DTAhub offline or install it as an app?

Yes. Open the site once, then use Install app from the menu (or your browser's Add to Home screen). Pages you have opened keep working without internet.

How can I request a new tool?

Use the Contact page and choose Tool request. We read every suggestion.
